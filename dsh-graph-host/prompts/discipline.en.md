**Agent Teams minimal contract (single-attempt fan-out + independent verifier; injected into the dispatch prompt only after the owner enables it):**
- **Fan-out members are not attempts**: within one attempt you may fan out **read-only or non-overlapping** work to several **members** (reusing the harness's native subagent/workflow primitives). A member gets no attempt ID, creates no worktree, occupies no board slot, and does not change the one-active-attempt-per-goal projection; the attempt lifecycle is the only closure -- every member must be either summarized or explicitly recorded as abandoned/timed out before the attempt ends, with **no dangling member**.
- **Limits and write discipline**: at most **N <= 3** members; do not fan out when there is no clearly independent parallel surface (single-threaded by default); write-type work must be partitioned (non-overlapping file sets) or serialized; members must **never** commit / branch / push on their own (the worktree discipline still applies), and they do not inherit the parent model route.
- **Independent verifier**: a member other than the author reviews some of the blocks; **an author's self-report must not masquerade as independent verification** -- the author's own output, self-check, and any review the author dispatched itself (recorded by g-436 as `self_requested`) **never count as independent verification**; independent verification must cite **role/source**.
- **Conclusion vocabulary (unified with g-436, closed set)**: `PASS` / `BLOCK` / `UNVERIFIED` (**never** FAIL).
- **Trace (tier A)**: write the fixed section inside the attempt completion summary under the heading `## Fan-out and independent verification` (the zh counterpart uses the corresponding localized heading documented in the supervisor guide), one entry per line with ASCII keys (replace every placeholder with real values; never copy the placeholders verbatim).
- member line format: `- member: name=<member> scope=<scope> status=<done|abandoned|timeout> conclusion=<PASS|BLOCK|UNVERIFIED> evidence='<one-line evidence>'`
- verify line format: `- verify: review=<review_id> scope=<reviewed block> conclusion=<PASS|BLOCK|UNVERIFIED> source=<independent|self_requested|author>`
- **Evidence reference**: `review` must point to a real trace at `<goalDir>/reviews/<review_id>.md` (the g-436 source of truth); **without a matching record you must not claim independent verification**; obey the results-face discipline (one-line evidence, never dump multi-line logs).
- **Degradation (fail-safe: never fail, only degrade)**: fan-out requires subagent depth >= 2; when the environment does not support it or the depth is < 2, **silently degrade** to the existing single-subagent path -- no error, no blocking -- and record the degradation reason truthfully in the results; when enabled but a member fails or times out, record the abandoned status truthfully and **never count it as PASS**.
